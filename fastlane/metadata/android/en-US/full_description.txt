Qalam Muslim is an open-source Islamic companion app built with Flutter. It brings Quran reading, Hadith, prayer times, Adhan, daily remembrance, Qibla, and practical worship tools together in an Arabic- and English-friendly interface.

<b>Prayer Times & Adhan</b>
• 14 prayer calculation methods with offline fallback
• Adhan playback with multiple reciters and per-prayer controls
• Pre-adhan reminders and configurable alerts
• Flip-to-silence and volume-key stop gestures

<b>Quran Reader</b>
• 10 Qira'at supported, including Hafs and Warsh
• Recitations with verse-synchronized audio
• Quran reading modes, bookmarks, and Arabic/English search
• Tafsir editions and memorization tools

<b>Hadith Library</b>
• 13 hadith collections
• Arabic and English texts with available grading
• Full-text search, explanations, and references

<b>Azkar & Supplications</b>
• Morning, evening, and daily remembrance
• 99 Names of Allah and digital Tasbih
• Custom remembrance lists and counters

<b>Additional Features</b>
• Daily and monthly prayer tracking
• Qibla compass
• Home-screen widgets
• Quran audio downloads
• Dark, light, sepia, and other theme presets
• Arabic and English interface with RTL/LTR support
• Offline-first access to core bundled resources
